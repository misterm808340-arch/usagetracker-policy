Privacy Policy

App: Usage Tracker Developer: Novarush Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Usage Tracker ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you install and use the app on your Android device. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. The short version

  • The app itself does not transmit any of your usage data off your device. All app-usage statistics, focus-session history, blocked-app lists, and daily limits are stored locally on your device in on-device databases.
  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its certified partners may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • The app does not require you to create an account, and no email, name, or password is collected.

2. Information collected by the app itself

Usage Tracker reads on-device usage statistics via Android's UsageStatsManager API to display per-app screen time, daily totals, weekly history, and trends. This data is:

  • Read from the device's system usage database (you grant this explicitly via Settings → Apps → Special access → Usage access);
  • Stored only in on-device storage (Room database + DataStore preferences);
  • Never uploaded, synced, or transmitted off the device;
  • Wiped completely if you uninstall the app or use the "Clear all data" option in the app's Settings.

3. Permissions the app requests and why

Permission Why we need it
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS Core feature. Read per-app usage time to show on the dashboard and in history charts.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW The blocker feature draws a "stay focused" overlay over user-selected apps.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE + FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Keeps the blocker overlay alive while you switch apps. Subtype: "App blocker overlay".
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show optional reminders (e.g. daily limit reached).
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restart the blocker service after device reboot.
ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY Optional Do-Not-Disturb toggle during focus sessions (off by default).
INTERNET + ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required by AdMob to fetch and display ads.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Required by AdMob to deliver personalized ads. The Advertising ID may be reset in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. Third-party services: Google AdMob

The app displays ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). When the app shows an ad, Google and its certified partners may collect or receive the following information:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID, limited-advertising-identifier signals);
  • Approximate device location (country-level, derived from IP);
  • App-interaction data (when an ad was shown, clicked, or dismissed);
  • Basic device information (model, OS version, screen size, language).

This information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Publisher Policies, available at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can opt out of personalized ads by resetting your Advertising ID or toggling "Opt out of personalization" in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Even with personalization off, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

5. Children's privacy

The app is rated "Everyone" (3+). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

6. Data retention

  • On-device usage data: kept locally until you uninstall the app or use "Clear all data" in Settings.
  • Ad-interaction data collected by Google AdMob is retained per Google's own retention policy; we have no access to it.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Withdraw any of the runtime permissions in Android Settings → Apps → Usage Tracker → Permissions;
  • Reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID;
  • Clear all data the app holds by tapping "Clear all data" in the app's Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted in the app's next Play Store release notes.
